# Operators basis

For the process $B \to K^{(*)} E_{\rm miss}$, this code implements the following operators. For $B \to K^{(*)} \nu \bar{\nu}$, the WET operators:

$$ \hat{C}_{\rm VL} (\bar{s}_L \gamma_\mu b_L) (\bar{\nu}_L \gamma^\mu \nu_L), \quad \hat{C}_{\rm VR} (\bar{s}_R \gamma_\mu b_R)(\bar{\nu}_L \gamma^\mu \nu_L),$$
$$\hat{C}_{\rm SL} (\bar{s}_R b_L) (\bar{\nu}_L^c \nu_L), \quad \hat{C}_{\rm SR} (\bar{s}_L b_R)(\bar{\nu}_L^c \nu_L),$$
$$\hat{C}_{\rm TL} (\bar{s}_R \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_L)(\bar{\nu}_L^c \sigma^{\mu\nu} \nu_L),$$

with the normalisation choice

$$ \hat{C}_i = \left(\frac{4G_F}{\sqrt{2}} \frac{\alpha}{2\pi} V_{ts} V_{tb}^* \right)C_i. $$

For $B \to K^{(*)} \phi$:

$$ C_{\rm SL} (\bar{s}_R b_L) \phi, \quad C_{\rm SR} (\bar{s}_L b_R) \phi.$$

For $B \to K^{(*)} V$:

$$ C_{\rm VL} (\bar{s}_L \gamma_\mu b_L) V^\mu, \quad C_{\rm VR} (\bar{s}_R \gamma_\mu b_R) V^\mu,$$

$$C_{\rm TL} (\bar{s}_L \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_L) V^{\mu\nu}, \quad C_{\rm TR} (\bar{s}_R \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_R) V^{\mu\nu}.$$

For $B \to K^{(*)} \phi \bar{\phi}$:

$$ C_{\rm VL} (\bar{s}_L \gamma_\mu b_L) (\phi^* \overleftrightarrow{\partial}^\mu \phi), \quad C_{\rm VR} (\bar{s}_R \gamma_\mu b_R) (\phi^* \overleftrightarrow{\partial}^\mu \phi),$$

$$C_{\rm SL} (\bar{s}_R b_L) |\phi|^2, \quad C_{\rm SR}(\bar{s}_L b_R) |\phi|^2.$$

For $B \to K^{(*)} \psi \bar{\psi}$:

$$ C_{\rm VL} (\bar{s}_L \gamma_\mu b_L) (\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu \psi), \quad C_{\rm VR} (\bar{s}_R \gamma_\mu b_R) (\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu \psi),$$

$$C_{\rm VL}^{\prime} (\bar{s}_L \gamma_\mu b_L) (\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu \gamma_5 \psi), \quad C_{\rm VR}^{\prime} (\bar{s}_R \gamma_\mu b_R) (\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu \gamma_5 \psi),$$

$$C_{\rm SL} (\bar{s}_R b_L) (\bar{\psi} \psi), \quad C_{\rm SR} (\bar{s}_L b_R) (\bar{\psi} \psi),$$

$$C_{\rm SL}^{\prime} (\bar{s}_R b_L) (\bar{\psi} \gamma_5 \psi), \quad C_{\rm SR}^{\prime} (\bar{s}_L b_R) (\bar{\psi} \gamma_5 \psi),$$

$$C_{\rm TL} (\bar{s}_R \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_L) (\bar{\psi} \sigma^{\mu\nu} \psi), \quad C_{\rm TR} (\bar{s}_L \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_R) (\bar{\psi} \sigma^{\mu\nu}\psi).$$

For $B \to K^{(*)} V\bar{V}$:

$$ C_{\rm SL} (\bar{s}_R b_L) V_\mu V^\mu, \quad C_{\rm SR} (\bar{s}_L b_R) V_\mu V^\mu .$$

For $B \to K^{(*)} \Psi \bar{\Psi}$:

$$ C_{\rm VL} (\bar{s}_L \gamma_\mu b_L) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho\gamma^\mu \Psi), \quad C_{\rm VR} (\bar{s}_R \gamma_\mu b_R) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho \gamma^\mu \Psi_\rho),$$

$$C_{\rm VL}^{\prime} (\bar{s}_L \gamma_\mu b_L) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho\gamma^\mu \gamma_5 \Psi_\rho), \quad C_{\rm VR}^{\prime} (\bar{s}_R \gamma_\mu b_R) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho\gamma^\mu \gamma_5 \Psi_\rho).$$

$$C_{\rm SL} (\bar{s}_R b_L) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho \Psi_\rho), \quad C_{\rm SR}(\bar{s}_L b_R) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho \Psi_\rho),$$

$$C_{\rm SL}^{\prime} (\bar{s}_R b_L) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho \gamma_5 \Psi_\rho), \quad C_{\rm SR}^{\prime} (\bar{s}_L b_R) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho \gamma_5 \Psi_\rho),$$

$$C_{\rm TL} (\bar{s}_R \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_L) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho \sigma^{\mu\nu} \Psi_\rho), \quad C_{\rm TR} (\bar{s}_L \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_R) (\bar{\Psi}^\rho \sigma^{\mu\nu}\Psi_\rho),$$

$$C_{\rm TSL} (\bar{s}_R \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_L) (\bar{\Psi}^{[\mu}  \Psi^{\nu]}), \quad C_{\rm TSR} (\bar{s}_L \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_R) (\bar{\Psi}^{[\mu}  \Psi^{\nu]}),$$

$$C_{\rm TPL} (\bar{s}_R \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_L) (\bar{\Psi}^{[\mu}  \gamma_5 \Psi^{\nu]}), \quad C_{\rm TPR} (\bar{s}_L \sigma_{\mu\nu} b_R) (\bar{\Psi}^{[\mu} \gamma_5 \Psi^{\nu]}).$$
